# Password Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes password requirements to protect company systems and confidential information.

## Requirements
- Passwords must contain at least 12 characters.
- Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords must not contain the employee's name or username.
- Passwords must be unique and must not be reused.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
All employees must enable MFA for company applications whenever available.

## Password Storage
Passwords must never be written on paper or shared through email or chat applications.

## Password Changes
Passwords should be changed immediately if compromise is suspected.

## Violations
Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action.
