# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely with manager approval.

## Working Hours
Employees must remain available from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time.

## Internet
Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection.

## Security
Only company-issued devices may be used for company work.
Employees must connect through the company VPN.

## Meetings
Employees must attend scheduled meetings unless approved otherwise.
